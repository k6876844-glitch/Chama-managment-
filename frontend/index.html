How it works

  1. Set up your chamaThe chairperson logs in and sets the contribution amount and loan interest rate.
  2. Add membersNames, phone numbers and roles: treasurer, secretary or member.
  3. Record as you goEvery contribution, loan and payout is one tap away.
  4. Share the reportExport a statement at each meeting so everyone sees the same numbers.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

In one central, secure database for your chama, so every official and member sees the same, up-to-date records from any device.

Who can see my records?

Only you and your chama officials. Every user logs in, and what you can see and do depends on your role.

How is loan interest calculated?

Interest is flat: a KES 10,000 loan at 10% means KES 11,000 to repay, whatever the term.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free.
